NMIS Access → GrowHub · Seedling planning

Seed and space calculation discrepancies

This page compares how NMIS Access and GrowHub calculate seed required, containers or bed feet, seedlings that will grow, and seeds per cell. It covers container and bareroot lots. Each case below gives the formula on each side, a worked example, what the difference does to the output, and a question for the SME.

Legacy sources: the saved queries and the NMIS_AGREEMENT_LOTS form VBA in nmis_Demo.mdb. GrowHub source: order-planning.component.ts and orders-main.component.ts in Nursery-GrowHub-Frontend, as of 16 Oct 2026.

How to read the worked examples

The examples use two illustrative lots, one container and one bareroot, with realistic inputs. They are not real lots. The lot export we have from the Oracle era is column-shifted (see M1), so its factors can't be trusted for a lot-by-lot replay yet. Every number was calculated with the exact legacy and GrowHub formulas, including their rounding.

Group 1

Container lots

C1

"Will grow" is capped at containers × cells in legacy, not in GrowHub

ContainerHigh

In legacy, a container lot can never "grow" more seedlings than it has cells. The Actual_Sow_Calculated query takes the smaller of the seed-based yield and containers × cells per container. That query feeds the sow order, the request summary and about 30 inventory and charge reports. GrowHub's seedling yield uses only the seed, so when a planner reduces the container count, GrowHub's yield and shortage don't change.

Legacy · Actual_Sow_Calculated query 
yield = SOURCE_REQUIRED × seeds/lb × germ × purity
        × HF1..HF4 (each rounded to 2 dp)
        × harvest ASF × thinning ÷ 1000
cap   = EST_CONTAINERS_REQUIRED × CELLS_PER_CONTAINER ÷ 1000
will grow = min(yield, cap)        (container stock only)
GrowHub · calculateSeedlingYield 
yield = round(seed × round3(factor1 × factor2 × factor3
        × germ × purity × thinning
        × past performance × seeds/lb))
No containers term.

Container example lot. Both sides use the same seed (2.194 lb). Only the container count changes.

C2

Container count: no query calculates it, legacy has three formulas, and GrowHub has a fourth

ContainerHigh

No saved query calculates EST_CONTAINERS_REQUIRED. Every report reads the value the planning form stored, and the form calculates it three different ways depending on which field the user edited last. So a legacy container count can't be traced to one formula. GrowHub uses its own formula. It replaces legacy container factors 1–3 and container ASF with "cells filled" ÷ "survival", it has no harvest factor 4 or harvest ASF (S2), and it rounds the factor product to 3 decimals before using it.

Legacy · form VBA (three paths) 
gross = round3(TOTAL_REQUEST ÷ (HF1..HF4 × harvest ASF))

A  Calculate button:
   gross×1000 ÷ (density × cells) × CF1×CF2×CF3 + 0.35
B  leaving Container ASF or CF1–3:
   gross×1000 × CF1×CF2×CF3 ÷ (density × cells) × container ASF
C  leaving Desired Density:
   gross×1000 ÷ (density × cells) × container ASF + 0.35
GrowHub · calculateCTREstimatedSpace 
a = round3(factor1 × factor2 × factor3 × past performance)
containers = round( (seedlings ÷ a) × cells filled
             ÷ (density × cells per container × survival) + 0.35 )

cells filled defaults to the grow cycle's containerFactor,
otherwise 100. Survival defaults to 100.

Container example lot (CF1 = 110 on the legacy side, cells filled = 110% on the GrowHub side).

Group 2

Bareroot lots

B1

Bareroot "will grow": the reports apply thinning, but the form and GrowHub don't

BarerootHigh

Legacy gives two different "will grow" figures for bareroot. The planning form's Source_Will_Grow leaves thinning out for seedbed (SB) stock. The Actual_Sow_Calculated query that the reports use applies thinning to every stock type. GrowHub follows the form. So GrowHub matches the planning screen the SME remembers but not the sow-order or request-summary reports.

      × germ × purity × past performance × seeds/lb))
No thinning, no HF4, no harvest ASF.

B2

Bareroot bed feet: legacy starts from the seed being sown, GrowHub from the seedlings requested

BarerootHigh

For seedbed stock, legacy calculates bed feet from the seed actually being sown (seed required × pure live seed). It recalculates whenever seed required changes, for example when seed is capped at the lot balance. GrowHub starts from seedlings requested, so bed feet don't move when the planner adjusts seed. GrowHub's function also computes germ × purity × seeds/lb and then never uses it. Its comment describes the legacy seed-based formula, but the code doesn't implement it.

Legacy · form VBA, SB branch 
bed feet = SOURCE_REQUIRED × PLS × CF1×CF2×CF3
           ÷ (density × cells per container) × container ASF
GrowHub · calculateEstimatedSpace 
bed feet = round( (seedlings ÷ (factor1×factor2×factor3
           × past performance)) × bareroot space
           ÷ (density × cells per container × survival) )

// comment in code:
// (Seed Quantity × Germination × Purity × Seeds Per Pound)
//   × Bareroot Space ÷ (Desired Density × Cells Per Container)
//   × Bareroot Survival

Bareroot example lot.

Container and bareroot lots

S1

Three different "seed required" figures

BothHigh

The seed the legacy form saves (SOURCE_REQUIRED, from the Calculate button) leaves thinning out. The seed-bank work order and source-calcs reports recompute it as SOURCE_CALC with thinning in. GrowHub includes thinning, like the reports, but has no harvest factor 4 or harvest ASF (S2) and adds past performance. Depending on which legacy figure the SME compares against, GrowHub can look either too high or too low.

S2

GrowHub has no harvest factor 4 and no harvest ASF

BothHigh

Legacy multiplies four harvest factors and a harvest ASF (an over-sow adjustment). GrowHub has fields for factors 1–3 only. Anything a lot carried in factor 4 or the ASF is lost, and this flows into seed, containers or bed feet, and will grow. In legacy, the seeds-per-cell "Use" button also back-solved values into factor 4, so factor 4 often holds a real adjustment.

Harvest factor 4 or ASFEffect in legacyEffect of dropping it in GrowHub
95 (container example)Seed and containers +5.3%Seed and containers 5% lower; will grow 5% higher
105 (bareroot example)Seed and bed feet −4.8%Seed and bed feet 5% higher; will grow 5% lower

Legacy: form controls HF1–HF4, Harvest_Factor, Calculated_Gross. GrowHub: planning form has factor1–factor3 only (order-planning.component.ts:967, 1012, 1073, 1229).

For the SME: does GrowHub need a fourth factor and an over-sow (ASF) field, or should those be folded into factors 1–3 when lots are migrated?

S3

GrowHub can't divide by a factor

BothHigh

Each legacy factor code has an operator. With * the factor multiplies (125 means ×1.25). With / it divides (125 means ×0.80). The form and every query honour this. GrowHub always multiplies, so a "/" factor moves the result the wrong way.

Factor 2 = 125, operator "/"Factor usedSeed relative to legacy
Legacy0.80100%
GrowHub1.2564% (36% too little seed)

Legacy: IIf([HFn_Formula_OP]="*", F, 10000/F) on the form, and the same test in SOURCE_CALC and Actual_Sow_Calculated. Operators come from NMIS_FACTOR_CODES.FORMULA_OP, which isn't in any export we have. GrowHub: no operator anywhere in planning.

For the SME: which factor codes use "/"? Were those values inverted before they were loaded into GrowHub?

S4

Legacy plans seed without thinning, then reports "will grow" with it

ContainerMedium

This is a legacy inconsistency, but it affects what the SME expects to see. The Calculate button sizes seed to meet the request without thinning. The reports then apply thinning when they calculate "will grow". So a container lot planned for exactly 50,000 shows a shortage in the legacy reports. GrowHub sizes seed with thinning, so the same lot shows no shortage in GrowHub's own calculation.

S5

Which lots count as bareroot

BothMedium

Bareroot and container lots take different formula branches, and each place decides "bareroot" differently. A lot can therefore get the container formula in one place and the bareroot formula in another.

WhereBareroot when the begin container is
Form Calculate and Source_Will_GrowSB only
Form Containers_Will_GrowSB, SPB, TB (B or N category)
Actual_Sow_Calculated querySB, or anything starting with TB
GrowHubGrow unit stock type is Bareroot: SB8, SPB, STB, TB5, TB9

Effect: a TB lot used the container formula on the legacy form (with +0.35 and gross × 1000) but the bareroot branch in the reports. An SPB or STB lot got the container cap (C1) in the reports. In GrowHub, all of these are bareroot.

Legacy: form controls Type_of_Container and Containers_Will_Grow, and the B_or_C column in Actual_Sow_Calculated Query. GrowHub: order-planning.component.ts:376 (isBareroot), grow-unit crosswalk.

For the SME: are transplant beds (TB) and seed production beds (SPB) planned as bareroot?

S6

GrowHub treats a missing or 0% germ or purity two different ways

BothMedium

When the planning form loads, a blank germ or purity becomes 100%. In the seed calculation, 0 is also treated as 100%, so seed is planned as if the seed were perfect. In the yield calculation, 0 stays 0, so yield is 0 and the whole request shows as shortage. Legacy refuses to calculate seed without test data ("No test data entered to calculate source").

S7

Rounding differences

BothLow

These are small but show up in side-by-side checks.

  • The Actual_Sow_Calculated query rounds each harvest factor to 2 decimals: 87.5% becomes 0.88, a 0.6% change per factor. The form and GrowHub don't.
  • Seeds per cell: same formula on both sides (seeds/lb × seed ÷ (containers × cells)), but legacy reports round to 1 decimal and GrowHub to 2.
  • GrowHub rounds the container factor product to 3 decimals before dividing, then rounds the container count to a whole number after adding 0.35. Legacy stores the count unrounded (820.85 in C2).
  • Grams to pounds: GrowHub multiplies by 453.6, the legacy balance query uses 453.59 and 0.002204623.

Legacy: Seed_Per_Cell in Agreement_Lot_Sow_Order, Seed_Bank_Work_Order_Report query. GrowHub: order-planning.component.ts:1285 (seedsPerCell), :1229.

S8

Legacy splits containers across agreements

BothLow

When an agreement lot serves several client agreements, the legacy sow order and confirmation reports show each agreement's share: containers × (amount requested ÷ total request), rounded to a whole number on the sow order and to 1 decimal elsewhere. Seed is split the same way. I didn't find an equivalent in GrowHub planning. If the SME is comparing a per-client figure, the totals may agree while the per-client numbers don't.

Legacy: Agreement_PCT_of_Total Query, Agreement_Lot_Sow_Order, Agreement_Lot_Confirmation_Query, Request_Summary.

For the SME: are the differences you're seeing on lot totals or on per-client lines?

Group 4

Migrated lots

M1

Migrated lots get wrong or missing factors, thinning and container inputs

BothHigh

If the lots showing discrepancies came over from the old system, the inputs may be wrong before any formula runs. The Oracle-era lot export (seed/NMIS_AGREEMENT_LOTS.json) appears to have its factor columns shifted, and the migration script reads those columns by name. Several planning inputs also aren't migrated at all, so GrowHub fills in its defaults.

Column in the exportWhat it actually holdsWhere it goes in GrowHub
HARVEST_FACTOR_2Blank on all but 22 of 8,149 lotsfactor2, so blank (treated as 100%)
HARVEST_FACTOR_4Equals DESIRED_DENSITY on 1,689 of 1,714 lotsNot migrated
CONTAINER_FACTOR_1, _2Cell counts (160, 112, 98, 240…)Multiplied into containerSpecifications.nurseryFactors
CONTAINER_FACTOR_31–5 (looks like seeds per cell)Same as above
Container lots: harvest factors, density, thinningBlank on 3,270 of 3,526 container lotsGrowHub defaults

Fields with no Access source in the mapping workbook, and the default GrowHub uses instead:

  • Thinning probability: defaults to 100%, so seed comes out lower than legacy SOURCE_CALC by the lot's thinning (85% thinning → 15% less seed).
  • Cells filled: the grow cycle's container factor, otherwise 100%. Lot-level container factor overrides are lost.
  • Survival: 100%.
  • Past inventory performance: blank (treated as 100%) until a source lot is picked, then set to 100%.

Migration: admin/bin/oracle-seedling-seed.ts:250–295, 465–483 (Nursery-GrowHub-Backend). Mapping workbook rows 26, 30, 32 and 33 (Planning). GrowHub defaults: orders-main.component.ts:110–126. nurseryFactors only appears in the seedling-bank report, not in planning calculations.

For the SME: were the lots showing discrepancies migrated, or entered fresh in GrowHub? If migrated, can you share one or two lot IDs with the legacy screen values so we can compare them field by field?

Next step: replay on real lots

The replay script (tools/calc_replay.py) runs every formula above on real agreement lots and reports, for each case, how many lots differ and by how much. It is built and runs, but on the current export its figures aren't trustworthy because of M1. It needs a clean export of these tables from the Access backend (NmisData.mdb):

  • NMIS_AGREEMENT_LOTS: all factors, density, thinning, stored seed and containers
  • NMIS_UNITS: cells per container
  • NMIS_FACTOR_CODES: the "/" operator for S3
  • NMIS_SPECIES_PRODUCT_CYCLES: begin container for each species product

The existing seedling-planning rules in the explorer (SP-01 to SP-18) cover the form-VBA side of several of these cases in more depth.
